Space Resources Week [link](https://www.spaceresourcesweek.lu/space-resources-week-2021)

I presented a work done in collaboration with the [Space Economy Evolution Lab](https://www.sdabocconi.it/en/faculty-research/labs-and-research-centers/see-lab-space-economy-evolution) in SDA Bocconi and the Sapienza University of Rome.
The work consists in a both tecnical and economic analysis with the objective of understanding if it makes sense from a business perspective for a private company to enter the domain of in space transportation, storage and distribution systems.
