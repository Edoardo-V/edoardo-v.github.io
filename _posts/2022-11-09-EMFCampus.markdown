On November 11 2022, together with the Cristian Giacomini, the CEO of Modefinance International and Emilio Colombo from Cerved, I held a seminar on *Machine Learning Applications in Finance: Credit Scoring, Real Estate and Algorithmic Trading*. I started with an introduction to machine learning and then explained two use cases of **Algo Trading with RL**. This seminar provided a practical overview of how AI is transforming various sectors of the financial industry.
