I was invited back as a guest lecturer at the [HEC Paris FinTech Summer School](https://www.hec.edu/en/summer-school/summer-programs/fintech-future-finance), where I taught *Quantitative Finance* on July 7th, 2026. This followed my 2025 course in the same **"FinTech: The Future of Finance"** program.

The lecture was built around one idea: **quantitative finance is not about knowing the future — it is about making better decisions when the future is uncertain.** The journey unfolded across seven parts:

**Can We Predict the Future?**
- From a 51% edge to a business: costs, asymmetry and risk
- Prediction is not trading

**Capital Markets Landscape**
- Banks and profit centers
- Market making, asset management and hedge funds, execution desks, prop trading
- The role of quants in each

**Financial Engineering Foundations**
- Stochastic processes, Black and Scholes, Monte Carlo simulations
- Optimal execution: Almgren and Chriss, Avellaneda and Stoikov

**Systematic Strategies**
- Trend, mean reversion and intraday seasonality — from intuition to backtested P&L
- Judging a strategy in the right order, and why backtests are dangerous

**Portfolio Optimization**
- Combining many imperfect strategies, diversification and the efficient frontier

**Machine Learning in Trading**
- Why financial ML is genuinely hard
- Supervised learning, validation by walking forward in time
- Reinforcement learning for FX trading

**Generative AI in Trading**
- Cross-sectional momentum, value and quality strategies
- LLMs as signal extractors — and why not to ask an LLM "what should I buy?"
- Multi-agent system design for equity trading
