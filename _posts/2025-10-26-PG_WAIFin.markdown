## Reflections on the *AI in Finance* Workshop at ECAI 2025, Bologna

On October 26, 2025, I had the pleasure of co-organizing the **AI in Finance Workshop** at this year’s *European Conference on Artificial Intelligence (ECAI)* in Bologna. The event exceeded all expectations: more than **80 participants** joined us for a full day of discussions, presentations, and exchanges at the intersection of artificial intelligence and financial innovation.

I had the opportunity to **moderate a panel** dedicated to the role of AI in the financial industry, featuring speakers from **Intesa Sanpaolo**, **Banco d’Italia**, and **Simitain**. Their perspectives on regulation, model adoption, and real-world deployment made the session one of the highlights of the workshop.

I also presented our recent work on **optimal execution using the Q-Reactive model**, discussing how reinforcement learning can improve execution strategies in modern electronic markets.

**https://sites.google.com/view/ECAI25-ai-fin**

The workshop generated great energy, insightful conversations, and a strong sense of community. Thank you to everyone who joined and contributed — looking forward to next steps and future collaborations!
