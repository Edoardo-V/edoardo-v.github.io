At the **EMAF 2020 conference on Mathematical and Statistical Methods for Actuarial Sciences and Finance**, 18-23 September 2020 ([link](https://www.unive.it/pag/40972/)), I presented my research work on risk-averse reinforcement learning and specifically the algorithm: *Trust Region Volatility Optimization*. This work proposes a novel approach to managing portfolio volatility using reinforcement learning techniques.
